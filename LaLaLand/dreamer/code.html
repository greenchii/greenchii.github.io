The Protagonist

They say this city is built on broken hearts and neon lights. I arrived with a suitcase full of sheet music and a vintage Leica, chasing the kind of magic that only happens between "Action" and "Cut."

My journey isn't just about the destination; it's about the bittersweet symphony of the climb. I find beauty in the flickering streetlamps of 2 AM, the crackle of a vinyl record, and the quiet moments before the curtain rises.
